THE BIG PICTURE —

Global problem.
Local possibilities.

Greenhouse gases in the atmosphere trap heat. Human activities such as burning fossil fuels, transport, industrial activity and land-use change have increased greenhouse-gas emissions. Tackling the problem requires action from governments, businesses, communities and individuals.

01

⚡ Energy

Electricity, heating and industrial processes can produce greenhouse-gas emissions when they rely heavily on fossil fuels. Energy efficiency and lower-carbon energy sources can help reduce them.

POWER & HEAT
02

🚗 Transport

Cars, aircraft, ships and other forms of transport often depend on fossil fuels. Walking, cycling, public transport and cleaner technologies can reduce emissions where they are practical.

HOW WE MOVE
03

🌱 Food & Nature

Agriculture, food waste, deforestation and land use all influence greenhouse-gas emissions. Protecting ecosystems and reducing waste can form part of the solution.
